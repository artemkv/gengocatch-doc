# Privacy Policy

## Effective as of November 1st, 2026

This policy explains how "GengoCatch" mobile application collects, stores, uses, transfers, and shares Personal Data from its users.

"GengoCatch" is a companion app that connects to a process running on your PC.
The app receives the screenshots from your PC main monitor, extracts Japanese text and displays the relevant vocabulary.

We don't require login, and we process all images locally.

## Data We Collect about You

### Personal Data

We don't collect any personally identifiable information.

### Usage Data

We don't collect any usage data.

## Handling the Screenshots

All screenshots are processed on your device.

## Security and Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access or delete.
